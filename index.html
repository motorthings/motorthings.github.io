How it works · AESOP Transformation OS

Ready to deploy, with the proof.

A model can't grade its own homework. AESOP runs the whole path: find out what the agent does, evaluate it, attack it, repair it and re-test, with a human approving each stage. Every step leaves evidence you can show someone.

The model reads and judges. Code does the arithmetic. That split makes scores repeatable and cheaper to run.

01InvestigateMap what your agent actually does through multi-turn interviews, even without its instructions.→ 02EvaluateIndependent judges and deterministic checks across your instructions, governance and live endpoint.→ 03Red teamAdversarial scenarios generated from the findings, then run live against your agent.→ 04Repair and re-testRewrites the agent's instructions from the evidence, and catches regressions before they ship.→ 05Trace analysis · before, during, after launchCode real conversations by hand, in testing and in production. Your reading becomes judges that prove each repair held, so the agent keeps improving after it ships.→
